What is Cancer Awareness?

Know the signs

Regular check-ups and noticing small changes in our body can help doctors find cancer early, when it is easier to treat.

Support each other

A kind word, a phone call, or going to an appointment together can give strong emotional support to people fighting cancer.

Healthy habits

Not smoking, eating more fruits and vegetables, moving our body daily and going for screenings can lower the risk of cancer.
